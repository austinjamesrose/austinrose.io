## Why This Exists

I'm the kind of person who checks theme park wait times even when I have no plans to visit. There's something fascinating about watching the morning rush, the afternoon lull, and the chaos of holiday weekends show up in the data.

I wanted a better way to visualize this data than what was available, so I built one.

## How It Works

A GitHub Actions workflow runs every 30 minutes, pulling live wait time data and storing it in a SQLite database. The Streamlit dashboard lets you slice the data by ride, hour, day of week, or date range.

## What's Interesting

The data confirms some intuitions, since mornings beat afternoons and weekdays beat weekends. It also surprises. A few rides carry wait patterns nothing like what you'd guess, and holiday seasons are the most fun to watch in real time.

It could help plan a real trip. Mostly it scratches a curiosity itch and gave me an excuse to build a data pipeline I would use.
